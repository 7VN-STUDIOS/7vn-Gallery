Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how 7VN Studios (a business registered in Nigeria, BN 8281310) handles personal information on 7VN Gallery, available at gallery.7vnstudios.com.ng. Questions: support@7vnstudios.com.ng.

1. What we collect

  • Photographer accounts: your email address and password. Passwords are stored in scrambled (hashed) form by our authentication provider, and we cannot read them. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email and basic profile details.
  • Your content: photos you upload, file names, gallery names, segment names and your cover photo choices.
  • Technical data: standard server logs kept by our hosting provider, such as IP address, browser type and time of visit. Your browser also stores a sign-in session so you stay logged in.
  • Clients viewing a gallery: clients do not create accounts and we do not ask them for personal details. Opening a gallery link creates the same standard server logs.

2. How we use it

We use this information to run the service: to sign you in, store and show galleries, send account emails (such as confirmation and password reset), prevent abuse, keep the service secure and answer support requests. We do not sell personal information and we do not show advertising.

3. Service providers

We rely on trusted providers to operate 7VN Gallery: Cloudflare (website hosting and photo storage), Supabase (accounts and database), Brevo (account emails) and Google (only if you choose to sign in with Google). Your data may be processed in countries outside Nigeria. These providers process data only to deliver their services to us.

4. Photos and private links

Each gallery has a private link. Anyone who has the link can view the photos and download them, so photographers should share links carefully. Links can stop working after the expiry date a photographer chooses. Deleting a photo or gallery removes the files from our storage.

5. How long we keep data

We keep account information while your account is active. Photos and galleries stay until the photographer deletes them or asks us to. Expired gallery links stop working, but the files remain stored until deleted. On request we will delete your account and its data, except where the law requires us to keep something.

6. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to certain uses, or withdraw consent. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), private storage and access controls to protect your data. No online service is completely secure, so please keep your own copies of important photos.

8. Children

7VN Gallery is not meant for people under 18 to use as account holders.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed, and significant changes will be announced on the site or by email.
